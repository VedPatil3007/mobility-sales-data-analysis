📊 Mobility Sales Data Analysis & Reporting
An end-to-end data analysis, cleaning, and reporting capstone project developed as part of the Coursera course 'Advanced Data Analysis and Visualization with Copilot in Excel'. This project demonstrates the practical application of AI-assisted data modeling, automated calculations, and executive-level business reporting using Microsoft Copilot and advanced Excel functions.

🎯 Project Background & Objectives
The primary objective of this project is to ingest, clean, and structure raw transactional records concerning global mobility product sales, converting them into an organized, dynamic reporting workspace. By leveraging AI-assisted workflows through Microsoft Copilot alongside standard data modeling practices, the project ensures robust data integrity, accurate currency conversions, and automated summaries that dynamically recalculate upon data modification.

🛠️ Methodologies & Technical Implementation
    AI-Assisted Workflow Integration: Utilized Microsoft Copilot in Excel to accelerate formula generation, troubleshoot data anomalies, and structure multi-sheet summary layouts.

    Data Cleansing & Standardization: Handled missing values, normalized speed and distance metrics, and structured raw logs into clean, unified analytical tables.

    Dynamic Calculations & Modeling: Built robust summary models using logical aggregation formulas (SUMIFS, COUNTIFS) rather than hardcoded metrics, ensuring that all regional and product reports remain fully reactive to incoming data updates.

    Structured Data Architecture: Designed with clear separation between raw transactional logs, lookup reference tables, and executive reporting layers to maintain a clean workbook hierarchy.

📈 Key Metrics & Performance Indicators Tracked
The workbook aggregates and tracks several critical business metrics to evaluate regional and product-level performance:

    Total Units Sold: Volume tracking across different mobility product categories.

    Total Revenue Generation (USD): Net financial performance calculated post-discounts across multiple global markets.

    Regional & Continental Distribution: Sales performance breakdown segmented by countries and continents to identify high-growth geographic regions.

    Product-Specific Performance: Comparative analysis across various mobility vehicle types.

    Share of Sales & Performance Ranking: Relative contribution of each country and product category toward total organizational revenue.
